# MyTaco AI - Complete Feature Reference for TaalCoach

## 22 Core Features (From Official PDF)

### VOICE & CONVERSATION (3 features)
1. **Real-Time Voice Conversations** - WebRTC live conversations (6 languages, 6 levels)
2. **AI Conversation Rescue** - Smart help during conversations, 2 suggested responses
3. **6 AI Language Coaches** - Alloy, Echo, Fable, Onyx, Nova, Shimmer

### GAMIFICATION & CHALLENGES (2 features)
4. **Seven Challenge Types**:
   - Brain Tickler (timed quizzes)
   - Micro Quiz (fast decisions)
   - Native Check (natural phrasing)
   - Error Spotting (find mistakes)
   - Smart Flashcard (spaced repetition)
   - Swipe Fix (correct sentences)
   - Story Builder (scrambled words)
5. **Hearts/Focus Energy System** - Separate pools per type, streak shields, auto-refill

### LEARNING & PROGRESS (2 features)
6. **AI-Powered Learning Plans** - Weekly schedules, skill breakdown, auto-progression
7. **Speaking Proficiency Assessment** - 60s test, 5 dimensions (pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary, fluency, coherence)

### VOCABULARY & ANALYSIS (2 features)
10. **Smart Flashcard System** - Auto-generated from conversations, SRS algorithm
11. **Conversation Analysis** - Color-coded transcript (green/yellow/red), grammar/pronunciation/vocabulary feedback

### MONETIZATION (1 feature)
12. **Three-Tier Subscriptions**:
    - Try & Learn: FREE (15 min/month, 5 hearts)
    - Fluency Builder: €19.99/month (150 min, 10 hearts)
    - Language Mastery: €39.99/month (Unlimited)

### ENGAGEMENT & NOTIFICATIONS (2 features)
13. **Push Notification System** - Practice reminders, streak alerts, achievements, milestones
14. **Streak Tracking** - Daily counter, badges, longest streak

### ACCESSIBILITY (3 features)
15. **Guest Mode** - 15 minutes trial without registration
16. **Topic Selection** - Pre-built + custom topics, web search integration
17. **Multi-Language Support** - 6 languages: English, French, Portuguese, German, Dutch, Spanish

### DATA & PRIVACY (2 features)
18. **Session Summary & History** - Full conversation archive, XP tracking
19. **Progress Export** (Web only) - PDF, CSV, JSON, ZIP formats

### SOCIAL (1 feature)
20. **Social Sharing** (Web only) - Progress images, achievement cards

### NEWS & ENGAGEMENT (1 feature)
21. **Daily News Practice** - Fresh articles daily, 7 categories, all languages/levels

### B2B (1 feature)
22. **Institution Portals** (In Development) - Admin dashboard, bulk enrollment, progress tracking
