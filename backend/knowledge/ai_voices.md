# AI Voice Characters - Complete Knowledge Base

## Overview
MyTacoAI offers 8 AI voice characters, each with unique personality traits, vocal characteristics, and teaching styles. Users can select their preferred voice from **Profile → AI Tutor Voice**.

---

## Available AI Voices

### 1. **Alloy** 🎯
- **Personality**: Balanced & Neutral
- **Description**: A balanced, neutral voice suitable for all learning contexts
- **Vocal Characteristics**:
  - Even-toned and professional
  - Clear enunciation without being overly formal
  - Moderate pace and pitch
  - Gender-neutral sound
- **Teaching Style**: Straightforward, factual, and objective
- **Best For**:
  - Learners who prefer minimal personality
  - Professional/business language contexts
  - Formal conversation practice
  - All CEFR levels (A1-C2)
- **User Profile**: Analytical learners, professionals, those who prefer traditional teaching methods

---

### 2. **Ash** 🌟 (DEFAULT)
- **Personality**: Warm & Encouraging
- **Description**: A warm, encouraging voice that provides gentle guidance
- **Vocal Characteristics**:
  - Soft, reassuring tone
  - Gentle warmth in delivery
  - Patient pacing
  - Friendly and approachable
- **Teaching Style**: Supportive, patient, celebrates small wins
- **Best For**:
  - Beginners (A1-A2) who need confidence building
  - Anxious learners or those with language learning anxiety
  - Learners who appreciate positive reinforcement
  - Comeback learners returning after a break
- **User Profile**: Beginners, anxious learners, those who need emotional support and encouragement

## How to Change Voice

**Navigation Path**: Profile → AI Tutor Voice

**Steps**:
1. Open the **Profile** tab (bottom navigation)
2. Tap **"AI Tutor Voice"** under Settings
3. Browse the 8 voice options with avatars
4. Tap to select a new voice
5. Tap **"Save Preference"**
6. Confirm your selection
7. Your new voice will be active immediately!

**Note**: Voice preference applies to ALL conversation sessions going forward.

## TaalCoach Voice Guidance

When users ask about AI voices, TaalCoach should:

1. **List available voices** - Mention all 8 with brief personality traits
2. **Understand current voice** - Know which voice user currently has
3. **Recommend based on context**:
   - User's CEFR level (A1-C2)
   - Learning goals (pronunciation, fluency, confidence, etc.)
   - Learning trajectory (plateauing → suggest Shimmer)
   - Personality match

5. **Personalize suggestions** - Based on user's actual progress data

## Common User Questions

### "What voice do I have?"
→ Check user profile for `selected_voice` field (default: "ash")
→ Show current voice with personality description

### "What voices are available?"
→ List all 8 voices with personality traits
→ Suggest trying the voice selection screen

### "Which voice is best for beginners?"
→ Recommend: Ash (encouragement), Echo (clear pronunciation), or Coral (friendly energy)

### "Which voice for pronunciation?"
→ Strongly recommend: **Echo** (clear & articulate)

### "I'm stuck in a plateau"
→ Recommend: **Shimmer** (dynamic & energetic to reignite motivation)

### "Which voice sounds professional?"
→ Recommend: **Alloy** (balanced & neutral for business contexts)

### "How do I change my voice?"
→ Provide navigation: Profile → AI Tutor Voice
